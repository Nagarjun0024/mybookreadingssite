Think and Grow Rich

Think and Grow Rich is a personal development and self-help book by Napoleon Hill. It was first published in 1937 during the Great Depression. The book outlines principles for achieving success and wealth, based on Hill's study of successful individuals of his time.

APJ Abdul Kalam turningpoint

Turning Points is an autobiography of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, the former President of India and a renowned scientist. The book chronicles his journey from a small town in Tamil Nadu to becoming one of India's most respected figures. It highlights his early life, education, and significant contributions to India's space and missile programs.

Power of Subconscious Mind

The Power of Your Subconscious Mind is a self-help book written by Joseph Murphy. First published in 1963, the book explores the concept of the subconscious mind and its influence on our thoughts, behaviors, and overall life experiences. Murphy emphasizes the idea that by harnessing the power of the subconscious mind, individuals can achieve success, happiness, and improved well-being.
